**Calculator behavior.** The beta used a 500-minute target and a separate action to populate the weekly sheet with the entered punch times. The weekly calculation parses flexible time and break inputs and applies the chosen rounding step to each calculated day. The saved record contains the report title and notes, schedule, computed total and display date. Its storage key, `timecard_calculator_history_v1`, became the input to the later migration. [Copy into Week](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/1c851ac05ef48843cc4b067696916c4963023a89/time_card_calculator%20%281%29.html#L448) · [Time parsing](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/1c851ac05ef48843cc4b067696916c4963023a89/time_card_calculator%20%281%29.html#L620) · [Legacy storage](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/1c851ac05ef48843cc4b067696916c4963023a89/time_card_calculator%20%281%29.html#L656)

**Alpha storage and rendering.** The alpha defines `punchly_today_state`, `punchly_history` and `punchly_settings`. Its History write path identifies a matching record by date, and missing preferences default to an 08:20 target and light theme. Review the chart and export files to see which parts of the early README were implemented in this snapshot. ## v4 · Alpha2 integration

**Evidence.** The maintained release history identifies Alpha2 as v4. Its HTML predates the later explicit numbered marker. [`6f08ba6`](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/commit/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8) · [Diff fce2952 → 6f08ba6](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/compare/fce2952d35a18085f786951d59e5ff59ab1f0186...6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8)

- **Entry points.** Alpha2 brings daily and weekly tracking into `index.html` and removes `Punchly.html`. Classic scripts load in dependency order. Shift math moves into `js/shift.js`. Settings, reminders, the web manifest and the service worker get their first dedicated files. [Script stack](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/index.html#L249) · [Worker release](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/service-worker.js#L1) · [`manifest.webmanifest`](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/manifest.webmanifest)
- **Shift math.** `Shift.measure` branches on full day, half day, leave and holiday. Half days round half the base target up to a minute. Full days use recorded lunch when available and the lunch limit for an estimate before a complete lunch. The target and accrued work remain separate values. [Shift calculation](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/js/shift.js#L36)
- **Data and migration.** Browser keys expand to include date-keyed drafts and weekly state. `normalizeHistory` distinguishes daily records from archived weekly reports. It imports old calculator reports from the same browser origin and records a migration marker. The first full-backup flow validates payloads and supports Merge and Replace. [Migration](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/js/storage.js#L41) · [Backup import](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/js/storage.js#L136)
- **Reporting and preferences.** Weekly rows support workday toggles, break deductions and rounding. History filters use calendar ranges. Canvas analytics has a seven-day rolling average, and the Excel path writes sortable date cells with the vendored SheetJS bundle. Reminders are checked while the page is open and use a local deduplication log. [Week math](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/js/app.js#L258) · [Trend](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/js/charts.js#L1) · [Excel dates](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/js/export.js#L47) · [Reminder deduplication](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/6f08ba65687b1ca3aa093c5012f47e7f52d361b8/js/reminders.js#L5)

## v7 · Daily logs and overnight support

**Evidence.** v7 release marker. [`bee0e32`](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/commit/bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55) · [Diff 1f2e536 → bee0e32](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/compare/1f2e5364ad153f5e4d5549e427852424386fb1ce...bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55)

- **Daily logs in Week.** The app reads dates, saved records and draft inputs together. `logInput` builds a shared day model, `matchingDraft` identifies an unchanged draft, and `logEntry` derives the visible status and work value. Week includes Monday-to-date entries and a seven-day Quick Log, alongside the manual report. [Day model](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55/js/app.js#L420) · [Week and Quick Log](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55/js/app.js#L466)
- **Overnight shifts.** The canonical date is the shift start. Subsequent clock times can roll into the next day. The result includes `endDayOffset`, while validation rejects a shift lasting 24 hours or more and a work segment exceeding 18 hours. Clock refresh handles work spanning midnight. [Overnight math](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55/js/shift.js#L36) · [Relative clock](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55/js/app.js#L589) · [Midnight refresh](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55/js/app.js#L383)
- **Compatibility.** Older Today state is read as a fallback for its matching date. Version 7 keeps archived manual weekly reports distinct from the rolling daily view. [Legacy Today fallback](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55/js/app.js#L443) · [Draft access](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55/js/storage.js#L102)

## v8 · Dates and desktop timer

**Evidence.** v8 release marker. [`791a90f`](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/commit/791a90f82a3da134db4a05d143c7fae5cde0b4ba) · [Diff bee0e32 → 791a90f](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/compare/bee0e32a785969795c2d36915fdde5e503c1db55...791a90f82a3da134db4a05d143c7fae5cde0b4ba)

- The new `Attendance.connect` owns month navigation and date selection. `readDayEntries` is the app boundary between the calendar and the daily-log model. Selecting a dated shift can open Today. History gains record search, and the attendance summary counts leave or holiday entries separately. [Calendar states](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/791a90f82a3da134db4a05d143c7fae5cde0b4ba/js/attendance.js#L11) · [Calendar data](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/791a90f82a3da134db4a05d143c7fae5cde0b4ba/js/app.js#L489) · [Open a shift](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/791a90f82a3da134db4a05d143c7fae5cde0b4ba/js/app.js#L531) · [Record search](https://github.com/Arvind595/Punchly/blob/791a90f82a3da134db4a05d143c7fae5cde0b4ba/js/app.js#L10)
- The mini timer is exposed on desktop. It uses Document Picture-in-Picture where supported and an in-page floating card otherwise. The app supplies live totals and estimated checkout through `timerSnapshot`. Mobile haptics are preference-controlled.
