          About & help

          About Punchly

          Punchly is a free, open-source time tracker for the web. Use it in a compatible browser on a phone, tablet, or desktop, and install it where your browser supports installation. It can work offline after its first online visit.

          What you can do

          • Record full-day, half-day, overnight, and no-lunch shifts, with work targets and lunch tracking.
          • Review weekly hours, attendance, holidays, leave, history, change logs, and statistics.
          • Export timesheets and charts, keep backups, personalize appearance, and use reminders or the desktop mini timer.

          Privacy and data

          Records stay in this browser; Punchly has no account, built-in analytics, or automatic sync. Clearing browser data can delete records. Backups have no Punchly password, so store them safely. App updates contact the host; the optional Satoshi font contacts Fontshare. You choose when to share or send support emails.

          Open source

          Source code is available under the Apache License 2.0. Included third-party assets have their own licenses. View the source.

          Punchly helps you calculate your daily punch-out time, track weekly working hours, and maintain monthly attendance records — all in one simple place. Use it on the web on your mobile, tablet, or desktop.

          Your data stays yours

          Your attendance records are stored locally in your browser. No account or registration is required.

          A quick reminder: Export regular backups to avoid losing records if you clear browser data or change devices.

          Your browser storage is not automatically synchronized across devices.

          Install Punchly for quick access from your home screen or desktop. You can continue in your browser and install later from Settings.

          Import a Punchly backup file (.json) to restore your records and preferences, or continue to set up a new profile.

          Profile details are optional. Leave the display name blank to use your device name.

          How to use Punchly

          1. Open Punch, choose your day type on the first punch, and tap the button for each punch that follows.
          2. Use Today to correct a time, set a half day, or mark leave.
          3. Review recent shifts in Week or open a date from Dates.
          4. Use Export for reports and Settings → Data & backups to keep a copy.
